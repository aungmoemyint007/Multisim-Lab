🚀 Multisim Circuit Designs - Cool Edition ⚡

Welcome to the Multisim Circuit Designs collection! This repo houses some slick digital circuit designs, ranging from multiplexers to flip-flops. All circuits were created using Multisim—the go-to tool for simulating and testing electrical circuits in a virtual environment.

🛠️ What’s Inside?

Here’s a list of what you’ll find in this folder:

4in2bitMUX.ms12: The 4-input, 2-bit Multiplexer—because who needs basic multiplexing?

decoder16.ms12: The mighty 16-line decoder that turns complexity into simplicity.

decoder4.ms12: The 4-line decoder—small but powerful.

Fulladder.ms12: Full Adder circuit for when you need to add more than just numbers.

FULLMUX.ms12: A full-on multiplexer to handle all the signals with style.

FullSubstractor.ms12: Subtract your way to success.

JKflipflopUsingD.ms12: The JK Flip-Flop made with a D-type twist for the cool factor.

There are also Security Copy versions of the files, because you can never have too many backups. 😉

🔌 Requirements

To get your hands on these files, you’ll need:

Multisim (v12 or higher): The tool to open, edit, and simulate these circuits.

📚 How to Use?

Download the files to your local machine.

Open them using Multisim—prepare to be amazed by the designs!

Simulate and modify the circuits to fit your needs.

If you’re learning, take notes and observe how these circuits interact!
